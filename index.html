Hi, I’m Amanda.

A grad student at NYU ITP in Brooklyn, designing products that feel warm and human.

Empathy is where I start. I walk through the experience myself and listen closely to the people living it, until I understand what they actually need.

I like to stay close to how things get built, so I prototype fast and build with AI-assisted code when needed.

Since 2024, I’ve been designing AI products, from an AI music platform at initi:AI to agent features at N7 Interactive. Right now, I’m also building my own startup.

Before this, I was a marketer who makes clothes.

More of me ↓

Before UX, I spent two years in brand marketing at ITOCHU Textile ↗, running social media for Gramicci ↗, TAION ↗ and Mountain Equipment ↗ in China. Watching how people actually choose a product still shapes how I design.

Outside of design, you can usually find me in dance studios, on a climbing wall, or on a badminton court.
