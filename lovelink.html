TypePersonal project

Brief

LoveLink is an iOS app for couples who live apart. AI friends offer emotional support when your partner can’t answer, and a shared virtual room lets two people build memories together — making the relationship more resilient and fulfilling.

Focus

Product Design · UX Research · UI Design · AI Interaction Prototype

In one line

Connecting long-distance couples through an AI companion and a shared space.

Overview

An app that helps long-distance couples stay close through AI companionship, shared plans and a space they build together.

Background

Long-distance relationships (LDRs) are common among people aged 18–35. Couples are separated by school, work transfers, long business trips, family care, or meeting online. Digital tools keep them connected across cities and countries, yet many still struggle.

Information architecture

Five tabs that cover the everyday of a long-distance relationship.

TabFeatures
ChatAI friends · Argue mode (avoid direct fights) · Voice message mode
RoomDesign the bedroom together · Credit system · Store & furniture mystery box
HomeBoth partners’ status · To-do list · Anniversary reminders · Surprise planning · Reunion countdown
TestDaily horoscope · Personality tests (MBTI / blood type) · Positive affirmations
MeProfile · Album · Unlink · Settings

Home is the center: open the app and you see your partner’s status, the distance between you, and the countdown to your next meeting.

Home & pairing

Home: see each other at a glance.

  1. 1

    Pair with your partner. Enter their LoveLink ID and your two spaces connect.

  2. 2

    Status at a glance. Reunion countdown, your partner’s local time, both to-do lists, voice messages and a 24-hour status — all on Home.

  3. 3

    Shared memory wall. Places you’ve been and photos you’ve taken form a wall that’s only yours.

  4. 4

    SOS in chat. When emotions run high, call an AI friend in one tap: ask for advice, start Argue mode, or switch to a voice message.

AI friends

AI friends as referees: talk to us before you fight.

Two AI friends, ELVO and VELO, are always on your side. In Argue mode they debate your complaint from both sides — one takes your feelings seriously, the other explains what your partner might be going through.

  1. 1

    Emotional support. When your partner is offline, an AI friend catches your feelings first — listening and responding like a friend.

  2. 2

    Argue mode. Say it to the AI first; it suggests a gentler way to put it, so you avoid a head-on fight.

  3. 3

    Personalized advice. Tips based on both partners’ personalities, horoscopes and chat history.

  4. 4

    SOS. One tap from any chat — a buffer for heated moments.

You“He forgot my birthday!”
ELVO“How could Rick forget Helen’s birthday? That’s just unforgivable!”
VELO“Rick might have been overwhelmed with work. It’s not an excuse, but the date may have just slipped his mind.”

Shared space

Build the home you imagine, together.

  1. 1

    Decorate the bedroom together. Pick the floor, wall color and bed — two tastes, one room.

  2. 2

    Earn credits with to-dos. Check off shared to-dos; spend credits on furniture mystery boxes.

  3. 3

    Shared fridge & recipes. See what’s in each other’s fridge and decide on dinner together.

  4. 4

    Phonograph. Leave a voice message for your partner to play when they wake up.
