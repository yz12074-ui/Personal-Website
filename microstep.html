Brief

Microstep is an AI-powered goal achievement app that helps users build sustainable habits through personalized micro-interactions and adaptive feedback loops.

Designed to make long-term goal completion feel natural and motivating, rather than overwhelming.

My Role

Lead designer for the mobile app, focusing on user experience, interface design, and motion graphics for habit formation.

Tools Used

Figma, Principle, After Effects

Challenge

Designing habit loops that feel effortless.

Approach

User Research

  • Interviews with 50+ users
  • Habit formation studies
  • Competitive analysis
  • Psychology of motivation

Design Principles

  • Micro-interactions for feedback
  • Adaptive UI based on progress
  • Emotional design elements
  • Minimal cognitive load
