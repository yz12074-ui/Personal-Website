SERVICE DESIGN

VIVA TOY —
Let old toys be loved again.

TypePersonal project
TimelineNov 2024 – Jan 2025
DeliverableService system + App

Brief

VIVA TOY is a repair and circular platform for electronic toys. Families can recycle old toys, send them in for repair, or pass them on to kids who need them. A membership and VIVA TOY CARE, an AppleCare-style protection plan, mean expensive electronic toys can be fixed when they break and find a new home when they’re outgrown.

Focus

Service Design · Business Model · UX Research · App Design

In one line

Every toy deserves to be repaired and loved again — less waste, and the memories stay.
