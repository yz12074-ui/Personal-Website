From clothes to products

What sewing taught me about UX

Pattern making → systems

Change one seam and every piece moves.

Pattern making taught me to see the whole structure before the details, the same way I approach a component system.

Fittings → usability tests

A fitting is a test on a real body.

However good a garment looks on paper, it changes once someone moves in it. Testing a product works the same way.

Fabric → constraints and feel

Every fabric has its own rules.

Drape, stretch and weight decide what is possible, like the rules and limits of every platform.
